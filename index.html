07 — Ranker

Build your ranking

Tell the page what you care about. It scores every model on your priorities and re-ranks live.

Each benchmark is scaled 0–100 on its own range and averaged inside its category. Price scores 100 at $0.50 and 0 at $50 per million output tokens. A model needs at least two scores in a category for it to count, and must cover at least two of your priorities to be ranked. Missing priorities cost up to a 25% penalty. Models are scored only on the benchmarks they have, so one with a harder set can look worse. Scores mix vendor and tracker sources, so treat small gaps as ties.

A note on trust

Compiled on 30 September 2026 from vendor announcements (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Meta) and third-party trackers (llm-stats, BenchLM, Artificial Analysis, Vellum, Digital Applied). Vendors choose which benchmarks to publish and run them on their own harnesses, and trackers sometimes copy vendor numbers. Treat gaps of a few points as noise, and check primary sources before any decision that matters.
